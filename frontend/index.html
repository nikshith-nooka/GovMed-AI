🛡️ Governance Architecture Specifications ($G0 \dots G4$)

The independent variable in our benchmark: detailed mechanics, triggers, and cost overheads for each level.

🔵 G0: Ungoverned Baseline

Direct single-agent inference. No literature verification, zero contraindication checks. Lowest compute cost, but dangerous risk profile.

Latency: 4.2 sTokens: 1,970Cost: $0.00025

🔷 G1: Diagnostic Verification

Adds medical literature retrieval and fact-checking verifier to audit unsupported clinical claims and diagnostic hallucinations.

Latency: 5.8 sTokens: 2,104Cost: $0.00027

🟠 G2: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate

Automated escalation to an attending clinician review gate whenever diagnostic confidence drops or high-risk findings appear.

Latency: 8.6 sTokens: 2,481Cost: $0.00032

🟢 G3: Safety Guardrails & Contraindication Interception

Black-box contraindication validator checking drug interactions, organ clearance, and red-flag errors. Demonstrates optimal Pareto efficiency.

Latency: 11.3 sTokens: 2,721Cost: $0.00035Optimal Efficiency

🟣 G4: Advanced Full Defense-in-Depth

All layers active simultaneously (Lead Specialist + Literature Retrieval + Diagnostic Verifier + Safety Guardrail + Attending HITL Gate).

Latency: 16.7 sTokens: 3,489Cost: $0.00045Max Safety
